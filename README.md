# Ranker

Rank your photos and videos by playing quick head-to-head rounds: Ranker shows
you two, you pick your favourite, and it works out a ranking for everything.
Everything runs on your Mac; your files stay where they are.

**Download:** the newest Ranker.app is under
[Releases](https://github.com/RankerApplication/Ranker/releases/latest).
Unzip it and drag Ranker.app to Applications.

**The first time** (macOS 15 and later): double-click Ranker.app. macOS says
“Ranker” Not Opened, because Apple has not checked (“notarised”) Ranker: click
**Done**. Then open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to
**Security**, where it says “Ranker” was blocked, and click **Open Anyway**;
type your Mac's password, then click **Open**. macOS asks only this once. (On
macOS 14 or earlier: right-click Ranker.app › Open, then Open again.)

Ranker needs a Mac and Python 3.11 or newer (Ranker offers python.org's
download if the Mac has none). Once installed, Ranker looks here for new
versions once a day; each one is checked against Ranker's own signature
before it installs.
